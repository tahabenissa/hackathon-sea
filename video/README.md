# SeaShield AI: demo video

- **`SeaShield_AI_demo.mp4`**: the narrated demo video (2 min 36 s, 2304×1296, 30 fps, AAC,
  −16 LUFS). It walks through the problem, how SeaShield checks every buoy reading, four live
  attacks on the real site running on a Vultr Compute instance, the real Hurricane Helene
  warnings going through, and the measured results with their limits.
- **`html/index.html`**: the same film as an interactive page (same source, same timing). Open
  it in Chrome or Edge, press Space to play, use the bar to scrub. Keep the folder structure
  (`film.js`, `player.js`, `build/`, `shots/`) next to it.

All screens of the dashboard are real captures of https://45-76-249-39.sslip.io, taken while
clicking the real attack console. Attacks are simulated on real NOAA data. The narration is an
AI-generated voice (Supertonic 3).
